Why I made this

Too many projects, too little memory.

I often found myself forgetting important details about projects, what version I was on, or when I last worked on it. Existing project management tools were either too complex or designed for teams.

Shadow is my solution: a lightweight desktop application that keeps all of my projects organized in one place while remaining fast and simple to use.

Everything a solo dev actually needs

Built to get you back into your work fast.

No team dashboards, no sprint boards, just the details that matter when you sit back down to code.

Launch straight into it

Run .exe or .bat files directly from Shadow. No more digging through nested folders to find the right file.

Filter by anything

Search projects by status, category, tag, or language, and find what you're looking for in seconds.

Track every version

Record which version you're on for each project, so picking back up never means guessing where you left off.

Log the stack

Note the languages, frameworks, and databases behind each project so the full picture is always at hand.

Real project status

Mark projects as Active, On Hold, Completed, or Discontinued, and always know what's actually worth your time.

Owners & collaborators

Keep track of who owns each project and who's helping build it, without leaving the app.

One click away

Every detail, right where you need it.

Version, stack, owner, timestamps, and links to GitHub, docs, or source, all in a single view.
